# Thirty-day build roadmap

Each release is intentionally small enough to inspect while remaining a coherent part of the game.

1. Product foundation, repository safety and delivery map
2. Deterministic simulation clock and canonical person model
3. New Life creation, exact date of birth and starting paths
4. Household generation, parents, school and childhood decisions
5. Grassroots football, local clubs, trials and safeguarding
6. Academy stages, age groups, promotion, release and reselection
7. Player attributes, development, training, fatigue and recovery
8. Fixtures, contextual match decisions and action resolution
9. Squads, selection, playing time, form and coach trust
10. Professional contracts, wages, bonuses and promised roles
11. Agents, representation, conversations and negotiation
12. Recruitment, club interest, transfers and renewals
13. Domestic leagues, tables, fixtures and promotion context
14. Continental competitions and qualification tracking
15. Awards, record books, honours and career statistics
16. Football news, interviews, media and public reputation
17. International eligibility, call-ups and association commitment
18. Citizenship, passports, visas and lawful residence
19. Airports, one-way travel, return travel and relocation
20. Education, qualifications, ordinary work and plan-B careers
21. Personal finances, taxes, debt, property and investments
22. Agents' support staff, social-media managers and advisers
23. Partners, marriage, separation and reciprocal relationships
24. Children, parenting, adoption and the persistent family tree
25. Personal brands, businesses, sponsorship and controversy
26. Injury, mental health, identity transition and retirement
27. Coaching, management, scouting, agency, media and business paths
28. Generational succession and autonomous relatives
29. Interface, accessibility, performance and long-save testing
30. Native macOS packaging, complete documentation and release audit

Every day includes a secrets check, build or validation step appropriate to the published slice, and a concise commit message explaining what changed.
